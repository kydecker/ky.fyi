My name is Ky Decker and you are on my website. Here are some facts about me:

- I currently lead design and development at [Namesake](https://namesake.fyi), a non-profit I co-founded to assist trans people in the US with legal name changes.
- Most of my job titles have included the word "designer", which I feel okay about.
- The browser is my favorite design tool. CSS is neat!
- I like working in code, but I try not to lose sight of the human problems that code and design are here to help solve.
- I have a degree in a somewhat-odd combination of Music and Computer Science. I like playing [piano](/arrangements) and noodling with synthesizers.
- I sometimes take [photos of birds and photos of non-birds](https://glass.photo/kydecker).
- I tend to a small (but growing) apartment balcony garden. My pothos is thriving.
- When I need to think about gnarly problems, I take my sketchbook and my pen, I find a quiet place to sit, and I write.
- I'm always open to chats and coffee. [Say hello!](mailto:hi@ky.fyi)

If you need the formal history, you can view [my resume](/resume).
